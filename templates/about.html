About Pink Penny

Pink Penny is a personal finance analyzer designed to help users understand spending habits, manage expenses, and build smarter financial routines through simple and meaningful insights.

Why Pink Penny?

Managing money can often feel overwhelming, especially when expenses are scattered across different categories and daily purchases go unnoticed. Pink Penny was created to make personal finance simple, accessible, and visually engaging.

By transforming financial data into easy-to-understand insights, Pink Penny helps users recognize spending patterns, improve budgeting habits, and work towards meaningful savings goals. The aim is not just to track money, but to help users build confidence in their financial decisions.
